About Me

Curious by nature, careful by habit

I am a Computer Science student at Mehran University of Engineering and Technology (MUET), Jamshoro, graduating in June 2028. My focus is artificial intelligence, and I am especially drawn to building practical tools that real people can use, such as intelligent assistants and applications powered by language models.

I believe good AI starts with good data. That is why I have spent my time getting strong with Python, Pandas, NumPy and SQL: cleaning messy data, asking the right questions of it, and explaining what it shows. I am now studying machine learning to take the next step from analysing data to building systems that learn from it.

Beyond code, I lead the MUET ACM Women Chapter as its President, where I help organise learning opportunities and build a supportive community for women in technology. I also enjoy design, and I use Figma and Canva to make what I build clear and pleasant to use.
